Lineage

Three roots. One fan-made fight.

Snack Ops 2 pulls its feel from a handful of games that shaped a generation of arena shooters, mixed into one SDK you can open, read, and build on.

01

Black Ops 2

The backbone: tight tactical gunplay, a readable HUD, and the stage-driven training rhythm that shapes how the SDK's zones and modes work.

02

NX1 · Future Warfare

The canceled Call of Duty that never shipped. Its futuristic direction informs the ballistics, the weapon sci-fi tone, and the "what if" spirit of the project.

03

Modern Warfare 3 · OG 2011

Small inspirational touches: the classic feel of a 2011 campaign-era shooter, carried into the death quotes, the kill feedback, and the menu energy.

Under the hood

Built for the feel.

Recent systems work across gunplay, movement, AI, and the HUD, tuned on Unreal Engine 5.8.

Unified TP / FP camera

Third- and first-person share one camera system with dynamic lag and clean mesh visibility splits.

Procedural ladders

Overhauled ladder system with tuned climb and detach behavior on BP_Ladder.

Stage zones

Labeled training zones for target practice and course legs, each with its own rules and messages.

Ballistics meshes

Millimeter-accurate bullet meshes for tracers and projectiles, sized to real calibers.

Death quotes HUD

A war-themed death-message array driving the HUD, editable through a simple paste workflow.

Navigation core

AI pathfinding and CMC navigation improvements, plus a ~30% physics cost reduction on UE 5.8.
